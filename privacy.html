The Short Version

Legacy Indie Radio is an independent radio station. The Legacy Indie Live app is free. It is ad-supported: starting with version 1.5.2 it shows ads served by Start.io (a banner at the bottom of the screen, and at most one full-screen ad when you open the app, no more than once every 45 minutes and never while music is playing). When an ad is requested, Start.io's ad software collects some device information to show and measure it — see Advertising below. You do not need to create an account to listen. Optional Legacy Indie Social accounts, for posting and messaging, are described under Legacy Indie Social Accounts below; you must be 16 or older to make one.

Apart from advertising, the personal information we handle is what you type into the app yourself — a chat nickname, chat messages, song requests, and dedications — plus a record of listening connections (IP address, player type, approximate location and listening times), described below.

What We Collect

Chat nickname. When you first open the app you choose a display name. It is stored on your own device and is attached to chat messages, requests, and dedications you send so other listeners know who they came from. Choose a nickname that does not identify you if you would rather stay anonymous.

Chat messages. Messages you send in the live chat are transmitted to our server, shown to other listeners, and kept in a short in-memory backlog so people who join can see recent conversation.

Song requests and dedications. The name and message you attach to a request or dedication are sent to the station and may be read on air or shown in the request queue.

Reports. If you report a chat message, we record the reported message, the nickname that sent it, and the nickname of the reporter, so the station can review it.

Listening connections. When you play the stream, our server records your IP address, the type of player or app you use (its “user agent”), the approximate city, region and country that IP address points to, and when you started and stopped listening. We use this to understand who is listening and from where, and to spot automated traffic. Location is estimated from the IP address only. We never use GPS or your device’s location. These records are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. We do not sell or share them.

Legacy Indie Social Accounts

Legacy Indie Social is the station's optional community at legacyindieradio.tailc6f66c.ts.net/social. You only give us this information if you create an account:

  • Handle, email address and password. Your handle is public. Your email is used to verify your account, reset your password and send account messages; it is never shown to other members. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash.
  • Date of birth. Asked once at sign-up to confirm you are 16 or older. We do not store your birthday, only the date you passed the age check.
  • Profile. Display name, bio and profile photo, if you add them. These are public.
  • Posts and reactions. Posts, photos and videos you attach, replies and “Turn it up” reactions are public. Before a photo or video is saved, the station removes the hidden details cameras add to the file, such as the GPS location, device model and dates. Videos (up to 10 minutes) are converted to 720p.
  • Private messages. Messages between members are stored on our server so both people can read the conversation. They are not public. Station moderators may review messages when it is needed to enforce the Community Guidelines or the law.
  • Moderation records. Bans, role changes, password resets and account deletions are kept in a moderation log.

Account data is stored on the station's own server; older videos may be kept on a storage drive attached to it. Copies of recent and popular videos and their preview pictures are also kept on Cloudflare R2, a storage service that delivers them to viewers and stores them only on our behalf. Account emails are sent through our Gmail address. We do not sell or share account data with advertisers.

What We Do Not Collect
  • Listening requires no account. An email address and password are only collected if you choose to create a Legacy Indie Social account.
  • No precise (GPS) location. The app never asks for location permission.
  • No access to your contacts, files, microphone, or camera. A photo or video is only uploaded when you choose one for your profile or a post. Videos are shrunk on your phone before they upload.

The app requests permission to post notifications purely so playback controls can appear in your status bar while the stream is running. Declining it does not stop playback.

Advertising

Version 1.5.2 and later of the app include the Start.io ad SDK. It shows a banner at the bottom of the screen (it never covers the player or the chat) and, when you open the app, at most one full-screen ad every 45 minutes — never on the first launch and never while the stream is playing. Every full-screen ad can be closed. Versions 1.5.0–1.5.1 contained Google's ad SDK but never showed ads; versions before 1.5.0 contain no advertising code.

When the app requests an ad, the Start.io SDK collects and sends to Start.io:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country, region, city).
  • Device and network information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, mobile carrier and network/Wi-Fi connection details.
  • Information about this app (its name and category) and your interactions with ads, such as impressions and taps.

The app does not ask for location, Bluetooth, contacts or other sensitive permissions, so the SDK cannot read your precise (GPS) location through this app.

Start.io uses this information to serve and personalize ads, measure them, cap how often you see them, and detect fraud, and shares it with advertisers and its advertising partners as described in the Start.io Privacy Policy. Listeners in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown non-personalized ads only.

You can opt out of personalized ads from Start.io at any time by emailing privacy@start.io or using the options in the Start.io Privacy Policy, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Stored On Your Device

Your nickname and your personal block list are stored locally on your device using browser local storage. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, erases them.

How Long We Keep It

Chat messages live in a limited in-memory backlog on the server and are discarded as newer messages arrive, and whenever the station software restarts. Requests and dedications are kept only as long as needed to play them and to maintain the station log. Reports are kept in a moderation log so repeat problems can be recognised.

Legacy Indie Social account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile, posts, photos, videos (including the copies on Cloudflare) and private messages (including conversations other members had with you). Your handle stays reserved so nobody can pretend to be you, and the moderation log keeps a record that the account was deleted.

Who We Share It With

When ads are requested, the advertising information described above is collected by Start.io and shared with advertisers and Start.io's advertising partners for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not sell, rent, or transfer anything you type into the app — your nickname, chat messages, requests, dedications, or reports — to third parties. Chat messages, requests, and dedications are, by their nature, shown publicly to other listeners — please do not put anything private into them.

Age

Legacy Indie Radio, the app and Legacy Indie Social are for people 16 and older. Account sign-up checks your date of birth and refuses anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Choices
  • Change your nickname at any time.
  • Edit or delete your Legacy Indie Social posts, and change your display name, bio and photo, at any time.
  • Delete your Legacy Indie Social account yourself from Profile → Delete account, or ask us using the data deletion page.
  • Block any user in chat — their messages stop appearing on your device.
  • Report any chat message for station review.
  • Clear the app's data to erase your nickname and block list.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads from Start.io by emailing privacy@start.io.
  • Ask us to delete anything you have sent, using the contact address below.
Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or privacy concerns: legacyindiesubmissions@gmail.com

We will respond to deletion requests within 30 days.
