How to request deletion

Have a Legacy Indie Social account? Log in at legacyindieradio.tailc6f66c.ts.net/social and choose Profile → Delete account. It happens instantly and removes your profile, posts, photos and private messages. Can't log in? Email us from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

For everything else (live chat, requests and dedications), email us and we will delete what you have sent to the station.

  1. Email legacyindiesubmissions@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Tell us the chat nickname you used in the app. That is the only way we can identify your messages, because we do not collect names, emails, or accounts.
  3. Say what you want removed: everything, or specific messages, requests, or dedications.
  4. We confirm and delete within 30 days of receiving your request.

After deletion we keep no copy, and nothing is passed to third parties — we never sold or shared it in the first place.

What you can delete yourself, instantly

Your nickname and your blocked-users list are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us, so we could not delete them even if you asked. To erase them:

  • Android Settings → Apps → Legacy Indie Live → Storage → Clear data, or
  • simply uninstall the app.
What is kept, and why

We keep nothing that identifies you after a deletion request. Two narrow exceptions:

  • Moderation records. If a message was reported and acted on, we may keep the record of that decision so the same abuse can be recognised again. It is retained no longer than 12 months.
  • Your handle. After a Legacy Indie Social account is deleted, the handle itself stays reserved so nobody else can take it and pretend to be you. Nothing else about you is kept with it.
  • Anonymous totals. Counts such as how many people were listening. These carry no nickname or message text and cannot be traced back to you, so they are not deleted.
What the app never collects

Listening and chat need no account, username, or password. An email address and password are only collected if you create a Legacy Indie Social account. No phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts, or files.

Advertising data

Version 1.5.2 and later of the app show ads served by Start.io (earlier versions show none). When an ad is requested, the advertising data Start.io's SDK collects (advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and network information, app information and ad interactions) goes to Start.io, not to us, so we cannot delete it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings, and ask Start.io to delete or stop using your data at privacy@start.io. See the Privacy Policy for details.
